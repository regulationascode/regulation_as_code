# Code of conduct

We welcome specific, respectful discussion from financial institutions, regulators, vendors, researchers, and individual contributors.

Be considerate in issues and reviews. Critique interpretations and code with evidence. Do not use personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, or disclosure of another person's private information. Do not post confidential institutional data.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates these expectations and may restrict participation. For abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's reporting controls. For a project concern that should not be public, request a private contact route without posting sensitive details.
